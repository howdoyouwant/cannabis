The short version

We're three people from the neighbourhood who got tired of shiny "lifestyle concept stores" charging €40 for a grinder and calling it curated. So we opened our own spot, painted it ourselves, and stocked it with things we'd actually buy.

No loyalty app. No corporate head office three countries away. No one's getting a bonus off your basket size. Just a shop, run by the people who work in it, in a city that's been doing its own thing for a very long time.
